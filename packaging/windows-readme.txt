CLAUDE MULTI-ACCOUNT LAUNCHER v{{VERSION}}
A product of Gerizon Technologies
==================================

Use several Claude accounts on one PC, each in its own tab.

SET UP
1. Unzip the whole "Claude Multi-Account Launcher" folder somewhere it will
   stay, such as Documents. Keep all the files together.
2. Double-click ClaudeMultiAccountLauncher.exe.
   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then
   "Run anyway". This release isn't code-signed yet.
3. It's now in your Start menu. Right-click it to pin it to the taskbar.

If you used "Claude Accounts" v2.0, your tabs and sign-ins move over
automatically the first time you open this version. You can then delete the
old folder.

ADD AN ACCOUNT
Click + (or press Ctrl+T), name the tab, then sign in inside it.
If Claude emails you a sign-in link, don't click it. Right-click the link,
choose "Copy link", then click "Paste sign-in link" in the tab.

STUCK ON AN ERROR PAGE?
If a tab ends up on another company's page (for example a connector sign-in
that shows an error), click "Back to Claude" in the blue strip, or use the
back (←) and home (⌂) buttons at the top right. Alt+Left goes back.

SWITCH ACCOUNTS
Click a tab, or press Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 or Ctrl+Tab.

DELETE AN ACCOUNT
Right-click its tab and choose "Delete account". This signs it out on this PC
and deletes its data here. The Claude account itself isn't deleted.

YOUR TABS ARE SAVED
Tabs and sign-ins stay after you close the app or restart the PC.

GOOD TO KNOW
- This is the web version of Claude. Desktop-only features such as Cowork
  aren't available.
- Browser extensions aren't supported.
- Google sign-in may not work inside apps. Use email sign-in instead.
